Privacy Policy

Dylan 11: Helix Hero, by Axiomar Studios. Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Dylan 11: Helix Hero (“the game”) uses, and why. The game is made by Axiomar Studios (“we”, “us”).

The short version

  • No account or sign-in is needed, and we do not ask for your name, email or location.
  • Your progress, settings and unlocked aliens are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. They only play when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward.
  • Any purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked aliens, upgrades, experience points, settings and your ad-reward progress) in the game's private storage on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob, a service from Google LLC, to show rewarded video ads. You only see an ad when you tap a “Watch Ad” button. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device information (such as model, operating system and language) and IP address,
  • ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped).

Depending on your consent and settings, ads may be personalised. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent and choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's consent message, where you can accept or decline personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from the game's pause menu, under Privacy & Ads.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on most phones: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • You never have to watch ads to play the game. They only speed up unlocking some optional aliens.

Purchases (Google Play)

If the game offers in-app purchases, they are processed by Google Play under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We only receive confirmation of which item was bought, so that the game can unlock it. We never receive your card or payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the Google services described above (AdMob and Google Play), the game does not send your data to anyone.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's own policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
